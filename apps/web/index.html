Why it can't be talked into paying a scammer

The language model
may only say no.

  • The model describes, rules decide.The amount must be in the message. A scam shape, or text aimed at the model, blocks the card, and nothing unblocks it.
  • The wallet comes from their profile.The recipient is the wallet on the requester's own Telegram profile. A different address in the message is refused.
  • Only the limits contract can spend.Your Gram sits in TonTech's official Agentic Wallet with its operator key switched off. Only our limits contract can spend from it, per payment and per day, on-chain.
  • Refusals cost nothing.The signing key lives on your phone. Refused payments never land on-chain and cost nothing.
