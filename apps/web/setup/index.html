Give your glasses an allowance

Your main wallet stays yours and is never touched by the glasses. You create a small wallet it owns, put some Gram in it, and set limits. Only your glasses can spend from it, and only up to those limits. You can pause or empty it from here at any time.

1 · Connect your main wallet

Gram Wallet in Telegram, Tonkeeper, MyTonWallet or any TON Connect wallet.

2 · Set the limits

3 · Create it, in one confirmation
